=== Serve 1: serve mechanics (first pass) ===

Legs
  hips drop while loading:      0.12 m
  deepest point of the load:    0.35 s before contact
  hips rise, stance to contact: 0.23 m (jump plus legs straightening)
  feet off ground at contact:   between 0.06 m and 0.28 m (see below)

Reach
  ball struck at:               2.60 m (from the trajectory fit)
  hitting wrist at contact:     2.09 m (right hand)
  shoulders at contact:         1.48 m

Toss
  peak height:                  3.64 m
  ball falls before contact:    1.04 m
  peak to contact:              0.48 s

Check: wrist to ball at contact = 0.51 m
  PASSED: consistent with a racket in the hand

How these were measured
  Joints from a pose model, placed on a vertical plane facing the camera.
  The plane starts where the server stands before the toss (6.1 m from the
  camera, from their feet on the court) and moves steadily to the fitted
  contact point (7.1 m) by the moment of contact.
  The player is 446 px tall in the frame. Joints were found in 100%
  of the frames analysed.
  Feet during the stance window moved 0.07 m.
  The feet-off-ground figure is a range because the feet are far below the
  camera, which makes their height sensitive to how far away they are. The
  upper value assumes the feet are still at the stance distance, the lower
  that they are level with the ball. They are somewhere between. The hips
  are much less affected.
  Not validated against any other measurement.
